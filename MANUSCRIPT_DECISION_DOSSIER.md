# Manuscript Decision Dossier

## 1. Current Evidence Summary

NeuroFate-Axis now has a coherent conservative story. SEA-AD provides strong endpoint-locked AD discovery evidence, led by the `neuronal_vulnerability_axis` (effect approximately -0.348, p approximately 0.0060, FDR approximately 0.0151). GSE174367 bulk RNA provides nominal independent AD replication for the same axis (n = 90; AD = 44; Control = 46; effect approximately -0.266, p approximately 0.0299, FDR approximately 0.2438).

PD evidence is informative but not confirmatory for a shared AD/PD axis. GSE243639 supports a preliminary cell/cluster-aware PD signal. GSE184950 and GSE20141 provide technically successful but weak or direction-only PD axis replication. GSE7621 is technically valid and shows a statistically strong opposite-direction `synuclein_mitochondrial_axis`, best interpreted as a candidate PD-divergent signal.

## 2. Primary Feasible Journal Targets

Primary target: eLife.

Backup target: NAR Genomics and Bioinformatics.

## 3. Why eLife Is Feasible

The project now has a clear computational systems-biology contribution: endpoint-locked donor/sample-level axis modelling that separates AD-replicated, preliminary cross-disease, and PD-divergent evidence. eLife is feasible because the story is biological, reproducible, cross-cohort, and explicit about limitations.

## 4. Why PNAS Remains Premature

PNAS remains premature because the shared AD/PD axis claim is not statistically supported. The strongest PD result in GSE7621 is opposite-direction synuclein--mitochondrial remodeling, which is interesting but divergent rather than shared replication.

## 5. Main Claim

NeuroFate-Axis is an endpoint-locked, leakage-aware donor/sample-level framework that identifies reproducible neurodegeneration axes across human brain transcriptomic cohorts. The strongest evidence supports an AD-replicated neuronal vulnerability axis, while PD cohorts show preliminary convergence and a candidate PD-divergent synuclein--mitochondrial axis.

## 6. Claims to Avoid

- Clinical diagnostic tool.
- Biomarker ready for clinical use.
- Causal mechanism.
- Validated shared AD/PD mechanism.
- Definitive disease mechanism.
- PNAS-level shared-mechanism claim.

## 7. Reviewer Risks

- GSE174367 AD replication is nominal but not FDR-robust.
- PD cohorts are small and heterogeneous.
- GSE7621 synuclein--mitochondrial signal is opposite-direction and requires careful explanation.
- Microarray probe aggregation may influence axis estimates.
- NeuroFate uses a compact curated target-gene panel rather than genome-wide pathway discovery.
