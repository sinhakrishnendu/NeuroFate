# Research-Use-Only Notice

NeuroFate is intended for research use only.

It is not validated for:

- clinical diagnosis
- patient-level decision-making
- treatment selection
- regulatory-grade biomarker deployment
- causal inference about disease mechanisms

Recommended language:

- research-use risk score
- donor/sample-level axis score
- cohort-level stratification
- endpoint-locked disease-state modelling
- external cohort validation framework

Avoid language that implies clinical deployment or individual care use. NeuroFate outputs should be reviewed as exploratory computational biology and bioinformatics evidence unless future clinical validation is performed under an appropriate protocol.
