# License And Data Use

NeuroFate is research software. It is not clinical software, a diagnostic device, or a clinical-grade decision tool.

External datasets are not bundled with this repository:

- SEA-AD data are external and must be obtained from the official source under the applicable source-specific terms.
- Mathys 2019 GEO files are external and must be obtained from GEO or linked official resources under the applicable source-specific terms.
- ROSMAP and AD Knowledge Portal resources may require Synapse access, data-use certification, or controlled-access approval.

Users are responsible for following all data-use, consent, attribution, redistribution, and publication requirements from each source dataset.

Generated NeuroFate outputs should cite both NeuroFate and the original data resources used to produce the analysis.
